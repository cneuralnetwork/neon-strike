Combat androids have taken the compound. Clear each wave, then use the 10 second break to grab pickups and reload.

Headshots deal double damage or more. Kills within 4 seconds of each other build a combo that multiplies points, up to x8.

The shotgun unlocks at 500 points and the sniper at 1500. Heavies are armored on the body, so aim for the head. Red barrels explode when shot and take nearby hostiles with them.

Green pickups heal, blue restore armor, amber refill ammo. Every fifth wave brings an Overseer.
